Agents manage the entire video production processBeta

In Carvit Create, AI agents handle every step in sequence, from performance analysis and topic ideas to video production and publishing on 6 channels.

Scenes from videos created and published with Create. All were generated by AI.

Agents run all six steps in sequence

Results from previous steps pass directly to the next, and if blocked, the cause is recorded and retried.

  1. 1

    Performance analysis

    Reads channel view and subscriber metrics to summarize which videos performed well.

  2. 2

    Topic suggestions

    Suggests topics from the candidate list based on programming criteria (safety, curiosity, mystery).

  3. 3

    Script and audio

    AI drafts and fact-checks the script, refines it, and adds voiceovers.

  4. 4

    Video cuts

    Generates cuts matching the script using AI video creation.

  5. 5

    Assembly

    Automatically assembles cuts, audio, and subtitles into a single video.

  6. 6

    Scheduling and posting

    Schedules and posts videos across 6 channels: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Facebook, and Naver Clip.

Humans review in only three places

The rest runs automatically. Review is required in only these three places.

  • TopicDecide what story to make.
  • AudioListen to and confirm the script and voice.
  • Assembled cutReview the finished video to decide whether to publish.

AI creates every scene

It generates an image for each scene, turns that image into a video a few seconds long, and stitches them together.
