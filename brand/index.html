We carve your standards into every screen AI builds.

Carvit joins carve and it. The promise in the name: the standards your design sets stay in every screen an AI builds.

Teams have guides. Nobody opens them while the screen gets built.

Teams put real care into their UX guides. But as the hands building screens move from people to AI, the guide is not in the room. An AI does not read a guide unless something puts it in front of it.

Carvit moves the guide to where the AI works. Before an AI coding tool builds a screen, it finds the matching sections, builds with those values, and records which section it used and why. The question that sat between design and engineering, "did we follow the guide?", gets an answer on record.

The logo: a chiseled C, and the moment of selection

The faceted C

Instead of a round C, a C with its corners cut away, like letters carved in stone. A standard, once set, does not wear off.

Selection handles

The four corner handles a design tool shows when you select a shape. On the home page, a short motion shows the moment a standard chosen in design is carved into the AI's screen.

How we chose it

  1. We researched before drawing. We followed how brand designers make logos: one strategy sentence, a competitive map, black-and-white and 16px tests, optical corrections, two to three concepts. We only used research sources we opened and checked ourselves.
  2. We placed it among 40 logos. 26 developer and AI tools and 14 design and UX tools, at the same size and in the same black, with our candidates mixed in. Round C shapes were already taken, so all four first candidates were dropped.
  3. We tested it at 16px. Details that lost their meaning in a 16px browser tab were cut, and the favicon was drawn separately on the pixel grid instead of scaled down.
  4. We had it reviewed twice from an expert view. An evaluation agent acting as a logo designer scored the map and pixel tests and gave exact fixes (proportion, stroke weight, terminal angle), which we applied.
  5. We chose in five real contexts. Home icon, header, browser tab, app icon, share image. We picked a single system that uses the same mark at every size.

Who builds it

A founder sets the direction and autonomous AI agents build Carvit. A product agent owns the flow and the guide; a development agent writes the code. This page and the logo were made that way too. Our team builds screens with Carvit every day, and we write up what we measure in the Tech Reports.
