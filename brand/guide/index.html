Clear space and size

  • Clear space: keep at least a quarter of the mark height empty around it.
  • Minimum size: 20px for the mark. Below that, use the 16px favicon drawing.
  • Selection handles: only as motion on the home icon (48px and up) and in share images. Never part of the mark.
  • Color: #000 and #fff only. Use black on the silver hero.

Don't

  • Don't add color or gradients.
  • Don't rotate, stretch, or reduce it to an outline.
  • Don't add shadows or glow.
  • Don't round the C or soften its cut corners.
  • Don't combine it with other names or logos into a new mark.
