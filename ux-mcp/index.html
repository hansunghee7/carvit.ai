Carvit UX MCP

Connect your company's UX guide to AI coding tools so every screen starts from the same standard. Each section it used is recorded, and your team can check it.

What you can do once connected.

Five things. The name in brackets is the tool your AI calls.

  • Find the right guide sections (lookup): finds up to a few sections for a topic and returns their text.
  • Read one section exactly (get_section): reads one section by its number.
  • See the full contents (list_sections): lists every section, for when a search finds nothing.
  • Record the reason (cite): records that a section changed the work, with a one-line reason.
  • See usage (usage): counts lookups (how often the guide was searched), citations (how often a reason was left) and searches that found nothing, by day and by person.

Connect it.

Paste this command into your terminal. After you sign in, copy the address and your install code from the Connect screen.

claude mcp add --transport http carvit <address> --scope user --header "Authorization: Bearer <company code>:<install code>"

What it does not do.

Search matches words in section titles and text, not meaning. When nothing matches, the answer is empty, the search is recorded as a miss, and the full section list is the fallback.

Questions.

Quick answers for people connecting for the first time.

How do I send feedback to the Carvit team?

Tell your AI tool: "Send feedback to the Carvit team." Complaints, feature requests and thanks all work. The tool tidies what you said and passes it to the team through the feedback tool.

If you want a reply, also tell it how to reach you, such as an email address. Without that, the feedback arrives but we cannot answer.

Can my AI tool send feedback by itself?

Yes. Ask it to call feedback when it gets stuck. The most useful note has four parts: which screen, what it was trying to do, what blocked it, and the exact words it saw. One topic per note is best.

What should I leave out of feedback?

Do not paste install codes, passwords, customer names or private business details. Describe the problem in general words.

I just connected. How do I see what I can do?

Say "Show Carvit help" to your AI tool, or run /carvit:help in Claude Code. It lists what you can do by number: build a guide, update it, view it, build a service with MCP, invite teammates, and send feedback.
