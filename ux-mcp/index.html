Carvit UX MCP

Connect your company's UX guide to AI coding tools. They look up the sections they need and record what they used.

What your AI tool gets.

Five tools it can call:

  • lookup Finds up to a few sections for a topic and returns their text.
  • get_section Reads one section by its number.
  • list_sections Lists every section, for when a search finds nothing.
  • cite Records that a section changed the work, with a one-line reason.
  • usage Counts views, citations and searches that found nothing, by day and by person.

Connect it.

Paste this command into your terminal. You receive the address and the secret key in your sign-up email.

claude mcp add --transport http carvit <address> --scope user --header "Authorization: Bearer <company code>:<secret key>"

What it does not do.

Search matches words in section titles and text, not meaning. When nothing matches, the answer is empty, the search is recorded as a miss, and the full section list is the fallback.
